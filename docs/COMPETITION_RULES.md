# Confirmed CCL behavior

These decisions were confirmed feature by feature during the October 2026 audit.

- One season is one tournament. The public default is the latest published tournament by start date across all cities, including future dates; creation time breaks equal dates. Drafts and archived tournaments stay hidden. Missing historical start dates use the earliest recorded fixture date. Overview uses the administrator's selected tournament.
- A club and a player each have a shared identity. Tournament entries, squads and results remain separate. Club registration has no player minimum. Each new tournament entry starts with an empty squad and requires at least five players, with no maximum. That minimum applies at entry only.
- Transfers lock automatically at the first scheduled kickoff, excluding postponed fixtures. Once the deadline passes, changing or deleting that fixture cannot reopen transfers. New people can still register; a person cannot switch clubs within that tournament. Removing and recreating an entry does not erase club history.
- Matches use eight players per side, including the goalkeeper, and two 25-minute halves plus stoppage time. Starting requires at least six; play stops below five. Staff decides the outcome of a stoppage.
- Select actual starters and substitutes for each match, using the club default as a starting point. Match selections lock once play starts. Allow five outfield changes and one goalkeeper change; no re-entry.
- New normal scores derive from goal, scored-penalty and own-goal events attributed to registered tournament players. Administrative forfeits are separate 3–0 decisions with a reason. Preserve imported historical results with a reconciliation label; never invent scorers.
- Every match in a published tournament is public. There is no separate match publication step. Played knockout draws go directly to penalties, with a decisive winner and no extra time.
- Points: win 3, draw 1, loss 0. Ties use head-to-head, overall goal difference, goals scored, fewer forfeits, lower disciplinary points (yellow 1, red 3), then staff-recorded drawing-of-lots order. Multi-club ties use a mini-table; reapply head-to-head to a remaining tied subset. Unresolved lots remain provisional.
- League/group schedules have one match per pair. Qualification counts and first-round knockout pairings are configured per tournament.
- Staff decides tournament-only suspensions case by case; yellow cards never automatically create bans. Specify a match count or an end date. Only completed played club matches with the player excluded from the whole match lineup serve a match ban; forfeits do not count. A date ban includes its end date, with return the following day. Eligibility checks remain outside the app.
- Stories, media and sponsors support tournament or global scope. New stories/media default to Draft. Scheduled stories publish at the chosen Istanbul date/time. Draft uploads remain private. Inactive sponsors lose branding without hiding media; deleting a sponsor retains media and removes its tag.
- Every visitor comment waits for manual approval. Automated screening only advises moderators. QA tournaments and test news intentionally remain public.
- Super admins have full control. Admins manage all areas but cannot grant or modify super-admin status. Editors manage stories/comments/media/sponsors and read standings, including drafts. Match operators manage competition records and read draft standings. Viewers use the public site only. Only super admins may change another super admin; the final super admin cannot be demoted or deleted.
- Production changes require separate approval for every change. Daily encrypted off-site backups with 30-day retention are planned; provider signup and activation are deferred by the owner.
